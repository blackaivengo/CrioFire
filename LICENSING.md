# Licensing: who can use CrioFire for free

CrioFire Private Firewall is **source-available**: anyone can read, build and change the code, and
it is free for most people. Businesses beyond the smallest pay for a yearly license per computer.
The exact terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE) (the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 plus CrioSoft's
additional permissions) and, for paying customers, [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md).
This page explains them in plain words; where they differ, the license texts win.

## Do I need to pay?

| You are... | Free? |
| --- | --- |
| A person using CrioFire on your own computers at home | Yes |
| A student, hobbyist or researcher, for non-commercial work | Yes |
| A charity, school, university or other educational institution | Yes |
| A public research, public safety or health, or environmental organization | Yes |
| A government institution | Yes |
| A freelancer or small business running CrioFire on **3 computers or fewer** in total | Yes |
| A business trying CrioFire out: up to 10 computers, once, for up to 30 days | Yes |
| A business running CrioFire on **more than 3 computers** | No: a license for **every** computer |
| An IT provider installing CrioFire on clients' computers as a paid service | No: a service provider license |
| Anyone selling CrioFire or including it in a product they sell | No: a separate agreement |

"Computers" counts every physical or virtual machine that runs CrioFire, across your company and
every organization under common control with it.

## What does a business license cost?

A subscription per protected computer per year, cheaper per computer in larger tiers, with every
update and email support included while it runs. See [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md)
for prices and terms, or contact [licensing email].

## Can I change the code or share it?

Yes, for the free uses above. You can change CrioFire and share your changed version, as long as
everyone who gets it from you also gets the license terms and the `Required Notice:` line from the
[LICENSE](LICENSE) file. Selling it, or using a changed version in a business beyond the free limits,
needs a commercial license.

## I work for a company and want CrioFire on my work PC

If your company runs CrioFire on more than 3 computers in total, it needs a business license that
covers your PC. Ask your IT department, or have them contact CrioSoft.

## Is CrioFire open source?

Not in the strict sense: open source licenses allow commercial use without paying, and CrioFire's
license doesn't. The code stays public, so anyone can check what a firewall on their computer does,
build it themselves, and report problems. Versions published before the license change
(November 2026) stay under GPL-3.0 for whoever received them.
